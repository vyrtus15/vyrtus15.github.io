Privacy policy

Effective 21 October 2026

Glowgrid is a free puzzle game for iPhone and Android, supported by ads. This policy explains what data the game and the services it uses collect, why, and the choices you have. Glowgrid has no accounts: you never give us your name or email to play.

Who we are

Glowgrid is made by Radu Proca, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom, who is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: glowgrid.support@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your device

Your level progress, best moves and stars, nickname, Nova's outfit, story choices and settings are saved only on your device. They aren't uploaded and we can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Your Player ID

On first launch the game creates a random Player ID. It isn't linked to your name, email or phone number. You can see it in the app under Profile, below the version. Analytics and crash reports (below) use it, so quote it when you contact us about your data.

Your choices

  • Ads and data consent: in the UK, EEA and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices on first launch. Change them at any time in Profile → Settings → Privacy options. If you decline, the game doesn't ask to track you and sends no analytics; ads may still appear, without personalisation.
  • Tracking on iPhone: the game only uses your advertising identifier if you allow tracking. Change it in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Analytics: switch off Profile → Settings → Share usage stats to stop sending analytics.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device settings.
  • US state privacy laws: where you have a right to opt out of targeted advertising, use Privacy options in the game (when shown) or your device's tracking and advertising settings.

How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you delete the game.
  • Analytics (PostHog): up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Crash reports (Sentry): up to 90 days.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to help you, and no longer than 2 years.
  • Google AdMob: under Google's retention policy.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (and the GDPR in the EEA) you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to how it's used, to receive a copy, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email glowgrid.support@gmail.com with your Player ID; without it we can't tell which data is yours. We reply within one month. For data Google holds for advertising, you can also contact Google directly.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or to the data protection authority where you live.

International transfers

Analytics data is stored in the EU. Google, Sentry and Expo may process data in the United States and other countries. These transfers are protected by the safeguards UK and EU law require, such as the UK–US data bridge, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Each provider is contractually required to protect the data at least as well as this policy does.

Children

Glowgrid is suitable for a general audience but isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. Ads are limited to content suitable for most audiences. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data sent by the game travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We only work with providers that protect the data they process for us.
