Mewlantern

A storm knocked over the town's firefly crates, and the lanterns went dark. Help Nova the cat push every crate back onto its glowing pad.

  • Crate-pushing puzzles: push, never pull, and mind the corners.
  • Ten chapters across the town, from the Garden to the Observatory.
  • A gift for Nova at the end of every chapter.
  • Hints whenever you're stuck, with no videos to watch.
  • Free to play with no ads in this version. No account needed.
