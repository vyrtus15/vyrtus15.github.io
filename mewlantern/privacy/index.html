Privacy policy

Applies to Mewlantern 1.1.0

Mewlantern is a free puzzle game with no account or cross-app tracking. Your progress, nickname and settings stay on your device. Your iPhone backup can include them with your other app data. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

Who we are

Mewlantern is made by Radu Proca, an individual developer and the controller of the personal data described here. You can reach us at mewlantern.support@gmail.com.

Gameplay analytics and crash reports

With your permission, the game sends information to PostHog so we can improve puzzles and fix errors. Gameplay information includes puzzle outcomes, hint use and hint timings with a temporary session identifier. Error reports can include the point in the code where an error occurred, the app version, device and system information, the current level and the names of recent game actions. Native iPhone crash reports can include an identifier created by the reporting service for this installation.

Turn Share gameplay analytics and crash reports on or off in Profile → Privacy. The setting starts off. Switching it off stops future reporting and clears the gameplay session identifier. We may be unable to associate an earlier session with you.

Feedback and support

If you tap Send in the in-game form, your message and the app version go to PostHog. Leave personal details out of the message. If you want a reply, you can email support. We receive your email address and the information you include when you email us.

How long data is kept

  • Local saved game stays until you delete the app or reset the relevant section.
  • PostHog keeps these events for up to one year and discards client IPs from stored events.
  • We keep support correspondence as long as needed to help you, up to two years.

Version 1.0.0

Version 1.0.0 uses Sentry’s EU service for crash reports and in-game feedback. Reports can include a Sentry installation identifier, device and iOS details, app version, level and recent game steps. Feedback can also include a name or email if you supplied one. Crash reports are retained for up to 90 days. Read the Sentry privacy policy.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law and the GDPR in the EEA, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, or receive a copy. Email mewlantern.support@gmail.com. Include the date, app version and any report reference or contact address you supplied so we can try to locate your data. We may be unable to identify a report that has no contact information. We reply within one month. Deleting the app removes its local data but does not automatically remove reports already sent.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or the data protection authority where you live.

International transfers and security

PostHog processes our events in the United States. Our providers may process service data in other countries, subject to their contractual safeguards for UK and EU data. Connections from the game use HTTPS. Access to diagnostic and support data is limited to running and supporting the game.

Children

Mewlantern is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has supplied personal data, contact us so we can investigate and delete it.
