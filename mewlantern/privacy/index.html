Privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026

Mewlantern is a free puzzle game. It has no advertisements, no tracking and no gameplay analytics, and no account is required. Data leaves your device in only three cases, described below: a crash or error report when something goes wrong, feedback you choose to send, and emails you send us.

Who we are

Mewlantern is made by Radu Proca, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom, who is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: mewlantern.support@gmail.com.

Your saved game

Your full saved game, including progress, best moves, stars, nickname, house, outfit, story choices and settings, stays on your device. The game has no cloud save or cross-device sync of its own; your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup includes it like other app data. Deleting the app removes its local saved game. The game does not send your nickname or upload a copy of your save. Crash reports can include recent game steps as described below.

Services the game uses

Service Data Purpose
Crash and error reports
Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., EU project) 		Sent only when the game crashes or hits an error: the error and where in the code it happened, a random identifier the crash reporter creates for this installation, device model, operating-system version, app version and build, the level being played, and the last game steps and app events (such as going to the background) before the problem. Taps are not recorded. Those recent steps stay on your device until a report is sent. The game sends no usage sessions or performance measurements. The app disables default personal information collection and overrides the reported IP address to prevent IP inference. The game never records your screen or takes screenshots. Finding and fixing crashes and errors.
Feedback you choose to send
Sentry		 Your message, with the same device and app details and recent game steps as a crash report; your name and email only if you choose to enter them. Those contact fields are optional. The form does not automatically fill them from your nickname. Investigating your report and replying when contact details are supplied.
Support email Your email address and the information you choose to send. Answering your request.

We use these services for our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and helping players. We do not sell personal data or use it for cross-app advertising. Apple may also provide aggregated store statistics and crash reports under its own privacy settings. The providers receive network addresses when connections reach them; the Sentry override concerns report content and does not make network connections anonymous.

On the App Store privacy label

Data linked to you, used only for app functionality and never for tracking: Name, Email Address, Customer Support, Other User Content, Device ID, Crash Data, Other Diagnostic Data and Product Interaction. Name and Email Address come only from feedback where you choose to enter them; Device ID is the crash reporter's random installation identifier; Product Interaction is the recent game steps in a crash report or feedback.

Your choices

You can play all of the puzzles, story and house content without an internet connection. Online support pages and sending feedback need a connection. Feedback is voluntary; avoid including sensitive information. The game never records your screen or takes screenshots.

How long data is kept

  • Local saved game: until you delete the app or reset the relevant section.
  • Crash and error reports: up to 90 days.
  • Support correspondence: as long as needed to help you, and no longer than 2 years.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law and the GDPR in the EEA, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, or receive a copy. Email mewlantern.support@gmail.com. Include the date, app version and any report reference or contact address you supplied so we can try to locate your data. We may be unable to identify a report that has no contact information. We reply within one month. Deleting the app removes its local data but does not automatically remove reports already sent.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or the data protection authority where you live.

International transfers and security

The Sentry project uses EU storage. Our providers may process service data in other countries, including the United States, subject to their contractual safeguards for UK and EU data. Connections from the game use HTTPS. Access to diagnostic and support data is limited to running and supporting the game.

Children

Mewlantern is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has supplied personal data, contact us so we can investigate and delete it.
