Privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026 · first iPhone release

Mewlantern is a free puzzle game. This version has no advertisements, advertising consent form, tracking prompt or gameplay analytics. No account is required. Advertising and analytics code is retained for a possible later update but is inactive in this release. We will update this policy before changing what the game collects.

Who we are

Mewlantern is made by Radu Proca, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom, who is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: mewlantern.support@gmail.com.

Your saved game

Your full saved game, including progress, best moves, stars, nickname, house, outfit, story choices and settings, stays on your device. There is no cloud save or cross-device sync. Deleting the app removes its local saved game. The game does not send your nickname or upload a copy of your save. Diagnostic reports can include recent game actions as described below. A PostHog Player ID is not created or displayed in this release.

Services used in this release

Service Data Purpose
Crash diagnostics
Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., EU project) 		Errors, diagnostic installation/session identifiers, device and operating-system details, app version and update, recent in-game actions, app sessions, and sampled performance data such as screen-loading times. The app disables default personal information collection and overrides the reported IP address to prevent IP inference. Session replay and automatic screenshots are off. Finding and fixing errors and performance problems.
Feedback you choose to send
Sentry		 Your bug report and associated app diagnostics; your name and email only if you choose to enter them. Those contact fields are optional. The form does not automatically fill them from your nickname. Investigating your report and replying when contact details are supplied.
App updates
Expo (650 Industries, Inc.)		 A random client identifier saved on this installation, platform, app/runtime version, update channel, current update and IP address when the game checks for or downloads compatible updates. If a previous launch failed, the updater can include a limited startup-error excerpt. Expo can derive aggregate app-usage statistics from update requests. This client identifier is separate from a PostHog Player ID and is not an advertising identifier. Delivering compatible fixes and operating the update service.
Support email Your email address and the information you choose to send. Answering your request.

We use these services for our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and helping players. We do not sell personal data or use it for cross-app advertising. Apple may also provide aggregated store statistics and crash reports under its own privacy settings. The providers receive network addresses when connections reach them; the Sentry override concerns report content and does not make network connections anonymous.

Your choices

You can play the included puzzles, story and house content without an internet connection. Updates, online support pages and sending feedback need a connection. Feedback is voluntary; avoid including sensitive information. This release has no gameplay-analytics switch because that service is inactive. A preference saved by an earlier preview is retained locally and does not enable collection. There is no recording of your screen or play session.

How long data is kept

  • Local saved game: until you delete the app or reset the relevant section.
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics: up to 90 days.
  • Support correspondence: as long as needed to help you, and no longer than 2 years.
  • Expo service data: under Expo's privacy policy.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law and the GDPR in the EEA, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, or receive a copy. Email mewlantern.support@gmail.com. Include the date, app version and any report reference or contact address you supplied so we can try to locate your data. We may be unable to identify a report that has no contact information. We reply within one month. Deleting the app removes its local data but does not automatically remove reports already sent.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or the data protection authority where you live.

International transfers and security

The Sentry project uses EU storage. Our providers may process service data in other countries, including the United States, subject to their contractual safeguards for UK and EU data. Connections from the game use HTTPS. Access to diagnostic and support data is limited to running and supporting the game.

Children

Mewlantern is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has supplied personal data, contact us so we can investigate and delete it.
