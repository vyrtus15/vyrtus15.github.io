How to play

Push every crate onto a glowing pad. Swipe, tap the square next to Nova, or turn on the on-screen arrows in Profile → Settings. Nova can push one crate at a time but never pull, so a crate pushed into a corner is stuck: use Undo or Restart. The full guide is in the game under How to play.

Where is my app version?

Open Profile and scroll to the bottom. This release has no gameplay analytics and does not show a Player ID. You do not need one to contact support.

I'm stuck on a level

Tap Hint for the next push. Hints are free and need no videos in this version. A hint shows where to walk, which crate to push and which way. A hinted solve earns up to two stars. Every level can be solved without hints.

I lost my progress / I have a new phone

Progress is saved on your phone only, so it doesn't move to a new phone and is deleted with the game. There is no cloud backup or automatic transfer. Email us the level you had reached and what happened; we can help investigate but cannot promise to restore a save.

Ads

The first release has no ads, and hints do not require videos. A dedicated later update may enable ads between levels and optional hint videos. If ads are enabled, report an inappropriate ad using Profile → Privacy options → Report an inappropriate ad, or email us with a description or screenshot.

Privacy choices and deleting your data

  • This version has no ads, tracking prompt or gameplay analytics. Open Profile → Privacy options for the current explanation and policy.
  • Crash diagnostics and app updates are described in the privacy policy. Sending feedback is optional.
  • To request deletion of a report, email us its date, app version and any contact details or reference you supplied. Deleting the game removes its local save; it does not delete reports already sent.
