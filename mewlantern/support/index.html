Help & support

Contact us: mewlantern.support@gmail.com

Please include your Player ID (see below), your phone model and what happened. In the game, Profile → Settings → Send feedback or report a bug fills these in for you.

How to play

Push every crate onto a glowing pad. Swipe, tap the square next to Nova, or turn on the on-screen arrows in Profile → Settings. Nova can push one crate at a time but never pull, so a crate pushed into a corner is stuck: use Undo or Restart. The full guide is in the game under How to play.

Where is my Player ID?

Open Profile and scroll to the bottom: it's under the version number. You can select and copy it.

I'm stuck on a level

Every level gets one free hint per attempt. After that, you can watch an optional video for another hint. A hint shows where to walk, which crate to push and which way. Every level can be solved without hints.

I lost my progress / I have a new phone

Progress is saved on your phone only, so it doesn't move to a new phone and is deleted with the game. Email us your Player ID and the level you'd reached, and we'll see what we can do.

Ads

There are no ads in the first five levels. After that, an ad may appear between levels every few levels, never in the middle of one. To report an inappropriate ad, use Profile → Settings → Report an inappropriate ad, or email us with a description or screenshot.

Privacy choices and deleting your data

  • Change ad and data consent in Profile → Settings → Privacy options (shown where the law requires it).
  • Stop analytics with Profile → Settings → Share usage stats.
  • To have your data deleted, email us your Player ID. Deleting the game removes everything stored on your phone.

Details are in the privacy policy.
