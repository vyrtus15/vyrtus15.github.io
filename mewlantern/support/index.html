How to play

Push every crate onto a glowing pad. Swipe, tap the square next to Nova, or turn on the on-screen arrows in Profile → Settings. Nova can push one crate at a time but never pull, so a crate pushed into a corner is stuck: use Undo or Reset. The full guide is in the game under How to play.

Where is my app version?

Open Profile and scroll to the bottom.

I'm stuck on a level

Tap Hint for the next push. Hints are free and unlimited. A hint shows where to walk, which crate to push and which way. A hinted solve earns up to two stars. Every level can be solved without hints.

I lost my progress / I have a new phone

Progress is saved on your phone, and the game has no cloud save or account of its own. It is included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup, so restoring a backup to a new phone (or moving with Quick Start) brings it along. Deleting the game deletes its progress. Email us the level you had reached and what happened; we can help investigate but cannot promise to restore a save.

Privacy and deleting your data

  • Mewlantern has no ads, no tracking and no gameplay analytics. Open Profile → Privacy for a short summary.
  • Data leaves your device only in a crash or error report (device model, iOS version, app version and your last few game steps) or in feedback you choose to send, which carries the same details plus any name or email you add. Sending feedback is optional.
  • On the App Store privacy label: Name, Email Address, Customer Support, Other User Content, Device ID, Crash Data, Other Diagnostic Data and Product Interaction. All are used only for app functionality, linked to you, and never used for tracking.
  • To request deletion of a report, email us its date, app version and any contact details or reference you supplied. Deleting the game removes its local save; it does not delete reports already sent.

Details are in the privacy policy.
